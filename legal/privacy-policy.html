Art Unbound - Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Art Unbound ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you play the App on Meta Quest devices. Art Unbound is a mixed reality puzzle game developed by Ajolote Studios (Ricardo Vazquez Armenta), based in Mexico. We are committed to keeping your data minimal and private.

1. Information We Collect

Art Unbound collects and uses only the minimum data needed to run the game and process purchases:

  • Meta Horizon User ID. When you make an in-app purchase, the App accesses your Meta-provided user identifier. This is used solely to associate the purchase with your account and to restore your entitlement (the one-time catalog unlock) across sessions and reinstalls.
  • Game progress data. Your puzzle progress, completion times, settings, and unlocked content are stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your headset.
  • Purchase records. In-app purchases are processed by Meta. We do not collect, store, or have access to your payment details (card numbers, billing information). All payment handling is performed by the Meta Horizon Store.

2. How We Use Information

  • To enable and restore your in-app purchase (unlocking the complete artwork catalog).
  • To save and load your game progress on your device.
  • To provide core gameplay functionality.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties. Art Unbound does not use external analytics services, advertising networks, or third-party data processors. The only data exchange is with the Meta Horizon platform itself, which is required to process purchases and verify entitlements (see Meta's own privacy policy at meta.com/legal/quest/privacy-policy).

4. Data Storage and Retention

Game progress is stored locally on your device and remains there until you uninstall the App or clear its data. Purchase and entitlement records are maintained by Meta on your account. We do not operate our own servers and do not retain copies of your data.

5. Children's Privacy

Art Unbound is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Your Rights

Because game data is stored locally, you can remove it at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing its storage on your device. For questions about purchases or entitlements managed by Meta, please refer to your Meta account settings.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
